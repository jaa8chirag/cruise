## Brand & Style

The design system reimagines elite private maritime voyaging through an ultra-luminous, Mediterranean sunlit lens. Reflecting the poise of private superyachts docked at Cap d'Antibes or navigating the Amalfi coastline, the interface captures the atmosphere of bespoke porcelain tableware, warm teak sun decks, and champagne flutes catching noon light. It targets ultra-high-net-worth travelers, private charter curators, and collectors of rare voyages who value calm, unhurried precision.

The aesthetic fuses **Warm Minimalist Grandeur** with **Subtle Tactile High-Luxury**:
- Sun-washed alabaster, soft cream porcelain, and ivory surfaces replace heavy dark tones with spacious air and quiet opulence.
- Gilded accents—champagne gold, burnished ochre, and warm metallic leaf—delineate visual weight with whisper-thin lines.
- Deep nautical charcoal and maritime navy typography ensure unyielding typographic contrast and effortless legibility.
- Physicality is established through warm, diffused ambient drop shadows and hairline bone-and-gold borders rather than flat or stark digital conventions.

## Colors

The light-themed palette captures daylight glistening on sea spray and polished brass. Primary accents rely on authentic metallic hues, framed by sunlit architectural surfaces and ink-deep maritime typefaces.

### Brand Metallic Accents
- **Primary Sovereign Gold (`#c59b27`)**: The principal interactive anchor, conveying heritage, sun-drenched brass, and bespoke service.
- **Champagne Gold (`#d4af37`)**: The radiant metallic accent for high-visibility focal points, hover highlights, and luminous button gradients.
- **Deep Ochre Gold (`#b8860b`)**: A grounded, high-contrast gold used for press states, fine badge borders, and accessible active links.
- **Pale Gold Wash (`#f9f5ea`)**: An ultra-soft champagne tint used for active chip selections, callout banners, and focused inputs.

### Surfaces & Foundations
- **Base Canvas (`#faf8f5`)**: Warm alabaster porcelain. Forms the primary viewport foundation, eliminating cold sterile whites in favor of linen-like warmth.
- **Pure Surface (`#ffffff`)**: Clean, crisp white for elevated cards, floating stateroom selectors, and contextual modals.
- **Ivory Structure (`#f3efe8`)**: Secondary container surface providing soft tonal separation for side rails, table headers, and structural backgrounds.
- **Sand Trim (`#ece6dc`)**: Deepest neutral surface tone, employed for recessed segments, segmented controls, and divider bands.

### Maritime Editorial Neutrals
- **Primary Ink (`#0e1724`)**: Deepest sea obsidian. Used for primary headlines, rates, and luxury titles to guarantee authoritative contrast.
- **Secondary Ink (`#1b2636`)**: Deep navy charcoal for body copy, navigational anchors, and stateroom nomenclature.
- **Muted Slate (`#4b5563`)**: Balanced neutral for supporting paragraphs, technical vessel specifications, and secondary descriptions.
- **Whisper Slate (`#8a94a6`)**: Form labels, unselected iconography, and nautical distance metrics.

## Typography

The typographical architecture juxtaposes the classical grace of editorial serifs with the immaculate readability of modern grotesque sans-serifs.

- **Playfair Display**: Applied to vessel series, destination names, quote excerpts, and pricing figures. Light and regular weights prevent visual bulk and preserve the look of fine letterpress. It must never be artificially italicized or forced into heavy bold weights.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans**: Manages functional utility across cabin selection, deck manifests, filtering mechanics, and technical yacht telemetry. Its geometric warmth complements the serif headings while preserving optical precision at small scales.
- **Tracked Capitals (`label-caps`)**: All structural indicators (e.g., "PRIVATE SALOON", "BERTH CAPACITY", "NAUTICAL MILES") employ elevated tracking (`0.18em`) and uppercase rendering to evoke bespoke shipbuilder plates.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a symmetrical, breathable 12-column fluid grid constrained by an 88rem (1408px) maximum boundary. It balances expansive photography with compact, structured operational matrices.

### Breakpoints & Geometry
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 20px margins. Reservation bars stack or dock as a bottom sheet.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px)**: 8 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 32px margins. Two-column suite comparisons and vertical chronological itineraries.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12 columns, 32px (`2rem`) gutters, 48px margins. Extended side-by-side deck plans, interactive reservation docks, and full-bleed editorial narratives.

### Spacing Cadence
Sections flow through large pauses (`space-4xl` and `space-5xl`), giving each yacht or destination room to breathe. Booking controls, guest pickers, and calendar grids collapse into compact increments (`space-xs` to `space-md`) for quick, confident interactions.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons heavy dark tints in favor of layered, warm porcelain planes, luminous soft shadows, and pale gold filigree borders.

1. **Sunlit Foundation (Level 0)**: Flat `#faf8f5` canvas.
2. **Elevated Porcelain (Level 1 - Voyage Cards, Cabin Modules)**:
   - Background: `#ffffff`
   - Border: 1px solid `#e8e1d5`
   - Shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(27, 38, 54, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(27, 38, 54, 0.02)`
3. **Bridge Floating Tier (Level 2 - Navigation Header, Sticky Booking Dock)**:
   - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)`
   - Backdrop Filter: `blur(16px)`
   - Border: 1px solid `#dfcaa0`
   - Shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(27, 38, 54, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.06)`
4. **Sunburst Modal (Level 3 - Deck Viewer, Fullscreen Gallery, Date Selector)**:
   - Background: `#ffffff`
   - Border: 1px solid `#c59b27`
   - Shadow: `0 24px 60px -8px rgba(14, 23, 36, 0.12), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.1)`

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Soft (Level 1)** curvature rhythm. This understated corner rounding conveys naval discipline and architectural permanence.

- Primary interactive elements, content cards, and media viewports employ subtle 4px to 8px radiuses (`rounded` to `rounded-lg`).
- High-touch interactive controls such as voyage filter chips and concierge availability badges use full pill geometry (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to stand out against structural rectilinear layouts.
- Dividers and decorative hairline accents maintain razor-sharp terminations, mirroring brass maritime instruments.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Sovereign Action**:
  - Background: Linear gradient from `#d4af37` through `#c59b27` to `#b8860b` (135deg).
  - Text: Pure White `#ffffff`, font weight 600, uppercase letter-spacing `0.08em`.
  - Border: None.
  - Hover: Ambient champagne glow `box-shadow: 0 6px 20px rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.35)`, translateY(-1px).
  - Active: Background shifts to `#b8860b`, shadow compresses.
- **Secondary Linen Action**:
  - Background: `#ffffff`.
  - Border: 1px solid `#dfcaa0`.
  - Text: `#1b2636`.
  - Hover: Border shifts to `#c59b27`, background fills with `#f9f5ea`.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Geometry: 32px height, full pill radius.
- Inactive: Background `#f3efe8`, border 1px solid `#e8e1d5`, text `#4b5563`.
- Active: Background `#f9f5ea`, border 1px solid `#c59b27`, text `#b8860b`, font weight 600.

### Input Fields & Reservation Pickers
- Background: `#ffffff`.
- Border: 1px solid `#e8e1d5`.
- Text: `#0e1724` with placeholder in `#8a94a6`.
- Label: Tracked caps (`label-caps`) in `#4b5563` floating cleanly above.
- Focus: Border shifts to `#c59b27` accompanied by an ambient halo `0 0 0 3px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`.

### Cards & Voyage Modules
- Surface: Crisp white `#ffffff` mounted on `#faf8f5`.
- Edge: 1px perimeter outline in `#e8e1d5`.
- Header: High-resolution nautical imagery treated with a faint ivory bottom vignette (`linear-gradient(to top, #ffffff, transparent 35%)`).
- Accents: Badges (e.g., "PRESIDENTIAL STATEROOM") sit in ivory pill frames bounded by hairline gold strokes.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Base: 4px rounded squares (checkbox) or circular rims (radio) in `#ffffff` with a 1.5px border in `#dfcaa0`.
- Selected: Solid `#c59b27` with a white checkmark or center pin.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Sunlit Voyage Dock**: A fixed-bottom horizontal booking bar rendered in frosted white porcelain (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.94)`) with gold hairline dividers separating Departure Port, Destination, Dates, and Stateroom Type, punctuated by the Sovereign CTA.
- **Regatta Itinerary Line**: A fine vertical or horizontal thread in `#e8e1d5` punctuated by hollow gold ring nodes (`#c59b27`), with current anchor days filled in champagne gold.
- **Deck Plan Stateroom Tooltip**: Floating porcelain card (`#ffffff`) with subtle gold framing (`#dfcaa0`) showing square footage, private veranda specifications, and real-time charter availability.
