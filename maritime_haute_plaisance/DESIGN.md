## Brand & Style

The design system evokes the discreet grandeur, stillness, and tailored exclusivity of private ocean yachting and world-class luxury expeditions. Designed for ultra-high-net-worth travelers, private suite collectors, and bespoke voyage curators, the interface mirrors the experience of stepping onto a midnight teak deck beneath open ocean constellations. 

The aesthetic is a hybrid of **Modern Minimalist Grandeur** and **Nocturnal Glassmorphism**:
- Deep ocean obsidian tones form an endless, immersive horizon.
- Translucent veil overlays, golden ambient light refractions, and hairline gold-leaf strokes establish spatial hierarchy without clutter.
- Restrained editorial layouts balance opulent display serif titles with crystalline, utilitarian typography for complex booking workflows.
- Micro-interactions are deliberate, frictionless, and velvety—never abrupt.

## Colors

The color palette centers on the infinite depth of open-ocean night skies contrasted against reflective metallic warm champagne gold.

### Primary Spectrum (Champagne Gold)
- **Primary Accent (`#d4af37`)**: The principal brand metal. Used for key interactive drivers, highlighted suite perks, and focus anchors.
- **Champagne Highlight (`#e6ca65`)**: Used for luminous hover states and gradient apexes.
- **Pale Starlight Gold (`#f3e5ab`)**: Used for delicate hairline borders, pill badge labels, and star ratings.

### Foundation & Oceanic Layers (Obsidian Abyss)
- **Base Canvas / Void (`#060d17`)**: Pitch ocean obsidian. The primary backdrop for immersive journeys.
- **Elevated Canvas / Trench (`#0b1528`)**: Deep nautical indigo for structural surfaces, cards, and floating panels.
- **Surface Elevation (`#112240`)**: Night tide blue for elevated interactive controls, suite modules, and search docks.
- **Translucent Overlays**: `rgba(11, 21, 40, 0.65)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` to emulate tinted marine glass.

### Tertiary & Metallics
- **Muted Bronze / Teak (`#8c734b`)**: Grounded warmth for structural dividers, secondary borders, and route metadata.

### Neutral & Contrast Accents
- **Text Dominant (`#f8fafc`)**: Brilliant crisp white for immaculate legibility against dark abyssal surfaces.
- **Text Subdued (`#94a3b8`)**: Cool nautical mist for supportive descriptions, nautical miles, and technical metadata.

## Typography

The typography pairing contrasts the literary grace of fine European publishing with the optical precision of contemporary luxury hospitality interfaces.

- **Editorial Serifs (`Playfair Display`)**: Applied to destination names, yacht suite categories, narrative overviews, and hero statements. It commands presence and introduces warmth, craftsmanship, and historic elegance. It must never be rendered in faux-italics or excessively heavy weights; light-to-medium strokes retain its haute couture profile.
- **Functional Grotesque (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers razor-sharp legibility across reservation engines, price schedules, stateroom floor plans, and cabin amenities. It remains clean, neutral, and unencumbered.
- **Tracked Micro-Labels (`label-caps`)**: All uppercase labels (e.g., "VERANDA SUITE", "PORT OF CALL", "DEPARTURE DATE") feature wide letter-spacing (`0.18em`) to mirror luxury house engraving.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored on an expansive **12-column fluid grid** housed inside a maximal centered container (`88rem` / `1408px`). It accommodates monumental widescreen imagery while ensuring precise alignment for dense reservation and itinerary selectors.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Columns
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 20px screen margins. Booking filters dock as floating glass drawer sheets.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 32px screen margins. Itinerary steps collapse into an interactive vertical accordion.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12 columns, 32px (`2rem`) gutters, generous 48px+ screen margins. Itinerary overviews, deck plans, and reservation rails coexist side-by-side.

### Spacing Philosophy
Generous vertical pacing (`space-4xl` to `space-5xl`) between narrative modules enforces an unhurried, prestige feeling. Reservation bars and cabin configurators tighten into `space-xs` through `space-md` grids for high-density spatial economy and decisive booking flows.

## Elevation & Depth

1. **Ocean Floor (Level 0)**: Solid `#060d17`. The foundation of the viewport.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Soft (Level 1)** shape language to maintain structural poise, architectural authority, and maritime discipline. 

- Interactive inputs, itinerary nodes, stateroom cards, and media vessels feature refined `4px` (`rounded-sm`) to `8px` (`rounded-lg`) corner treatments.
- Curved silhouettes are subtle, never bubbly or cartoonish, echoing the sleek, hydrodynamic architecture of modern megayacht hulls.
- Floating category filter pills and promotional status badges utilize full pill radii (`9999px`) purely for contextual contrast against rectilinear architecture.

## Components

### Buttons & Actions
- **Primary Sovereign CTA**: 
  - Surface: Linear gradient from `#d4af37` through `#e6ca65` to `#c59b27` (angle: 135deg).
  - Text: Deep Obsidian `#060d17`, font weight 600, uppercase letter-spacing `0.08em`.
  - Border: None.
  - Hover: Ambient starlight glow `box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.45)`, slight translateY(-1px).
- **Secondary Glass Action**: 
  - Surface: Translucent obsidian `rgba(17, 34, 64, 0.5)` with `backdrop-blur-md`.
  - Border: 1px solid `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`.
  - Text: `#f8fafc`.
  - Hover: Border shifts to `#f3e5ab`, background fills to `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.1)`.

### Reservation Inputs & Suite Selectors
- Background: Smoked ocean slate `rgba(11, 21, 40, 0.7)`.
- Border: 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- Label: Tracked mini-caps (`label-caps`) in `#94a3b8` perched 6px above or inline.
- Focus: 1px solid `#d4af37` accompanied by a soft golden peripheral halo (`0 0 12px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`).

### Cards & Voyage Tiles
- Encapsulated in 1px gold-tinted hairline borders (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`).
- Media headers use a gentle dark ocean gradient mask (`linear-gradient(to top, #0b1528, transparent)`) to weave photography seamlessly into content metadata.
- Suite card badges (e.g., "OWNER'S PENTHOUSE", "EXCLUSIVE CHARTER") appear as semi-opaque obsidian chips bounded by gold thread.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Elements
- Custom custom-cut squares (4px radius) in deep obsidian with hairline bronze-gold outlines.
- Active State: Filled with champagne gold `#d4af37` featuring an ocean-navy tick icon.

### Chips & Filters
- Compact height (`32px`), pill-shaped silhouette.
- Inactive: Background `rgba(17, 34, 64, 0.4)`, text `#94a3b8`, border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Active: Background `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`, text `#f3e5ab`, border `1px solid #d4af37`.

### Bespoke Domain Components
- **Floating Voyage Anchor Bar**: A desktop/mobile floating reservation dock pinned to the screen bottom, containing date selection, port of embarkation, stateroom suite type, and real-time live fare quotes with a gold gradient checkout button.
- **Port Itinerary Timeline**: Vertical/horizontal nautical thread accented with luminous gold ring nodes denoting days at sea, ports of call, and private zodiac excursions.
